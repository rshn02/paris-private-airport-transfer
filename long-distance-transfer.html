Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about booking a private chauffeur for long-distance journeys from Paris.

How far can I travel with a private chauffeur from Paris?

We provide long-distance private transfers to numerous destinations throughout France. Cross-border journeys to selected European destinations are also available on request, subject to availability for your itinerary.

Can you pick me up from an airport or Disneyland Paris?

Yes. Pickups can be arranged from Paris, Île-de-France, CDG Airport, Orly Airport, Beauvais Airport and Disneyland Paris, as well as hotels, residences, train stations, private addresses and other agreed pickup locations within our service area.

Can I book a one-way, round trip or private day journey?

Yes. One-way transfers and return journeys can be arranged. A private day journey may also be possible when the destination, planned itinerary and time available allow it.

Can we include several stops during the journey?

Rest stops can be arranged during long journeys. Additional itinerary stops may also be possible when agreed in advance and compatible with the planned journey and time available.

Can I travel with a lot of luggage?

Yes. Vehicle selection can be adapted to the number of passengers and amount of luggage. Please provide accurate passenger and luggage information when requesting your quote.

What information should I provide for a long-distance quote?

Please provide your pickup location, destination, travel date and pickup time, number of passengers, luggage, requested stops and whether you need a one-way, round trip or private day journey. Your quote will be prepared around the itinerary and requirements provided.
